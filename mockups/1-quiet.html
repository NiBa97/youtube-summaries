How it works

01

Paste a link

Any YouTube URL — a talk, a podcast, a three-hour deep dive you were never going to watch.

02

We read the transcript

The full transcript is condensed into a fixed six-part form: thesis, key points, a headline number, the quote, timeline, closer.

03

You read, then verify

Every key point links to its timestamp. If a claim smells off, you're one click from the source — at the exact second.

What's in it

Files itself
Each video is sorted onto one of your topic shelves and tagged. Suggested new tags wait for your approval — the library never grows behind your back.
Community check
One button reads the comment section against the summary. If commenters credibly dispute a claim, that claim gets a caveat. Most videos get nothing — that's the point.
Reading states
Unread, reading, read — plus a star. Your queue behaves like an inbox, not a feed.
Yours
Self-hosted, single user, no accounts. Summaries and transcripts live in your own database and re-render offline.
