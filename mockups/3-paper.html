The routine

Paste, wait half a minute, read.

1.Paste any YouTube link — the three-hour interview you bookmarked in March qualifies.

2.The transcript is fetched and condensed into the same six-part form every time: thesis, key points, a headline number, the quote, a timeline, a closer. Sameness is the feature — your eyes learn where everything lives.

3.The summary files itself onto one of your topic shelves. Suggested tags queue for your approval; nothing is added to your library without a nod.

Particulars

Small courtesies.

Trust, then verify

Every key point is time-stamped into the source. A dubious claim is one click from its origin.

The commentariat

On request, the comment section is read against the summary. Claims the audience credibly disputes receive a printed caveat.

An inbox, not a feed

Unread, reading, read, starred. No autoplay, no thumbnails, nothing suggested. The queue holds only what you gave it.

Entirely yours

Self-hosted and single-user. Transcripts are kept, so your library re-renders offline, forever.
