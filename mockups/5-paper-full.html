The routine

Paste, wait half a minute, read.

Twelve hours of video becomes forty minutes of reading. Skim or read; star or skip.

First,

paste a link.

Any YouTube URL — the three-hour interview you bookmarked in March qualifies. Captioned videos in any language work.

Then,

it is condensed.

The transcript is distilled into the same six-part form every time: thesis, key points, a headline number, the quote, a timeline, a closer. Sameness is the feature — your eyes learn where everything lives.

Particulars

Small courtesies.

The things you would build yourself, given a quiet weekend.

One-shot summaries

Drop in any URL, subscribed or not. Half a minute later it is a summary in your queue.

An inbox, not a feed

Unread, reading, read, starred. No autoplay, no thumbnails, nothing suggested. The queue holds only what you gave it.

Deep-linked moments

Every bullet carries a timestamp into the original video. Verify, don't trust.

Shelves and tags

One topic shelf per video, tags beneath it. Machine-applied tags are marked and revertible in bulk.

The commentariat

One button reads the comments against the summary and prints caveats where the audience credibly objects.

Entirely yours

Self-hosted, single user, no accounts. Transcripts are kept, so the library re-renders offline, forever.
